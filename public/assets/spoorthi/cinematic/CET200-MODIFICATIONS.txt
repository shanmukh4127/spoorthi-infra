Changes in this distribution:
- Render-only static meshes are grouped by material to reduce draw calls.
- Non-render collision geometry and manufacturer lettering are omitted.
- The original detailed crawler belt is placed on both sides of the machine.
- Paint and surface treatments are adapted for the illustrative environment.
- Positions and normals use standard KHR_mesh_quantization. Visible mechanical
  geometry is retained without triangle decimation.
